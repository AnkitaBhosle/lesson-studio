## Plan
1. Hook — "Why does money left alone keep growing faster?"
2. Interest on interest — Compound interest means you earn interest on your interest, not just on the money you put in. (Source: Investor.gov)
3. Watch $100 grow — At 5% a year, $100 becomes $105, then $110.25, and passes $162 in 10 years. (Source: Investor.gov)
4. Give it more time — Leave it 25 years and it's almost $340, without adding a cent. (Source: Investor.gov)
5. The Rule of 72 — Divide 72 by the rate to estimate the years to double; at 9%, about 8 years. (Source: Investor.gov)
6. Time is the key ingredient — The earlier money starts growing, the more of the total comes from interest. (Source: Investor.gov)
7. Try it yourself — "Plug your own numbers into the free Investor.gov compound interest calculator." On screen: the calculator page.
