## Plan
1. Title — A tour of the eukaryotic cell.
2. Eukaryotic cells have a nucleus — Their DNA is enclosed in a membrane-bound nucleus, unlike prokaryotic cells. (Source: Biology 2e, 4.3)
3. Ribosomes build proteins — Ribosomes are where proteins are made, free in the cytoplasm or attached to the rough ER. (Source: Biology 2e, 4.3)
4. The endoplasmic reticulum — Rough ER helps make and modify proteins; smooth ER makes lipids. (Source: Biology 2e, 4.4)
5. The Golgi apparatus — Sorts, tags and packages proteins and lipids to send them where they're needed. (Source: Biology 2e, 4.4)
6. Mitochondria — Mitochondria capture sunlight and use it to make sugar through photosynthesis. (Source: Biology 2e, 4.3)
7. Chloroplasts — In plants and algae, chloroplasts carry out photosynthesis using the green pigment chlorophyll. (Source: Biology 2e, 4.3)
8. Plant cells have both — Plant cells have mitochondria as well as chloroplasts. (Source: Biology 2e, 4.3)
9. Plant vs animal cells — Plant cells also have a cell wall and a large central vacuole. (Source: Biology 2e, 4.3)
10. Recap — Match each organelle to its job; exit ticket.
