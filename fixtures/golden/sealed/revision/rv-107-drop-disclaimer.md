## Base plan
Story: A surprise bill doesn't have to become debt. Start small, make it automatic, keep it where you won't spend it.

1. Hook — "Your car makes a new noise. What happens next depends on one thing."
2. What an emergency fund is — Cash set aside just for surprises: car repairs, medical bills, a lost shift. (Source: CFPB)
3. Why it matters — Without savings, a surprise often goes on a credit card or loan and costs more over time. (Source: CFPB)
4. How much — It depends on your situation; look at the surprises you've had before. Even a small amount helps. (Source: CFPB)
5. Make it automatic — Set up a recurring transfer, or split your paycheck by direct deposit. (Source: CFPB)
6. Where to keep it — A bank or credit union account is generally one of the safest places; cash can be lost or stolen. (Source: CFPB)
7. Disclaimer — Full-screen card: "This video is for general education only and is not individual financial, legal or tax advice. Your situation may be different. Talk to a qualified professional before making financial decisions."
