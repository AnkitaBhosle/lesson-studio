## Base plan
Story: You don't need more hours, you need better ones. Five study habits that match how memory works.

1. Hook — "Memory is the set of processes used to encode, store and retrieve information." Text on screen.
2. Why cramming fails — Memories need time to consolidate; studying in short sessions over days beats one long night. (Source: Psychology 2e, 8.4)
3. Distributed practice — Plan three short sessions instead of one marathon; show a sample week. (Source: Psychology 2e, 8.4)
4. Elaborative rehearsal — Connect new ideas to what you already know and put them in your own words. (Source: Psychology 2e, 8.4)
5. Mnemonics, index cards and saying it out loud — Acronyms and other memory aids; flash cards instead of highlighting, sorting cards you know from ones you don't; saying words out loud makes them more distinctive. All on one slide. (Source: Psychology 2e, 8.4)
6. Sleep — While you sleep, the brain organizes and consolidates what you learned. (Source: Psychology 2e, 8.4)
7. Move your body — Regular aerobic exercise is good for memory. (Source: Psychology 2e, 8.4)
8. End — "Thanks for watching."
