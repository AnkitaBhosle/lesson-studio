## Base plan
Story: Heat illness runs from mild to deadly. Know the signs, know what to do, and speak up early.

1. Hook — Title card: "Heat can make you sick at work." Photo of the warehouse floor. Narration: "This summer, the heat is part of the job. Here's how to stay safe."
2. Why it matters — Heat illness ranges from heat rash and cramps to heat stroke, which can kill without fast treatment. (Source: CDC NIOSH, Heat-related Illnesses)
3. Signs of heat exhaustion — Headache, nausea, dizziness, weakness, heavy sweating, thirst, peeing less. Icons for each sign. (Source: CDC NIOSH, Heat exhaustion)
4. What to do for heat exhaustion — Move to a cool spot, take small sips of cool water, take off extra layers, don't leave the person alone, get medical help. (Source: CDC NIOSH, Heat exhaustion first aid)
5. Signs of heat stroke — Confusion or slurred speech, passing out, seizures, hot dry skin or heavy sweating, very high body temperature. (Source: CDC NIOSH, Heat stroke)
6. Heat stroke is a 911 call — Call 911, stay with them, move them somewhere cool, cool them fast with cold water or ice on the neck, armpits and groin. (Source: CDC NIOSH, Heat stroke first aid)
7. Recap — Know the signs, stop early, cool down, and tell your shift lead. Card with the shift lead's extension.
