## Source: handout
DENSITY LAB - 7th gr, Unit 2

Materials per table: balance, ruler, 100 mL graduated cylinder (GLASS - careful), 3 blocks (wood, aluminum, plastic), 1 small rock, paper towels, goggles

Steps
1. predict: will each one sink or float in water? write it down
2. mass each object on the balance (g)
3. blocks: measure L x W x H with ruler (cm) -> volume in cm³
4. rock: fill cylinder to 50 mL, drop rock in gently, new level - 50 = volume (1 mL = 1 cm³)
5. density = mass / volume. show work
6. test: put each in the tub. were predictions right?

Safety: goggles on the whole time. glass cylinders stay in the middle of the table. spill = tell me + paper towels. nothing goes in your mouth.

Wrap up Qs (pick 2??)
- why does the rock sink but the wood float?
- if you cut the wood block in half what happens to its density?
- which has more mass, a kg of feathers or a kg of rocks (maybe too silly)
