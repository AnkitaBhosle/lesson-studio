## Source: notes
level 3 - pres perf vs past simple. 2 hrs w/ break but this part ~1 hr

- warm up: "What did you do last weekend?" (past) -> then "Have you ever...?" see if they notice
- FORM have/has + pp. irregular list again (go-gone, eat-eaten, see-seen, be-been)
- 3 uses only:
  experience (ever/never) - Have you ever been to NY? I have never driven a truck.
  still true (for/since) - I have worked here for 3 yrs / since 2022
  just/already/yet - She has just left. Have you eaten yet?
- BIG ONE: finished time = past simple. yesterday, last year, in 2019, ago
  X I have seen him yesterday ✓ I saw him yesterday
- Find someone who... (has ever...) speaking 10-15 min
- pres perf continuous NOT tonight, someone will ask, park it
