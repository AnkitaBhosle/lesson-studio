## Source: notes
nose breathing - why (beginners ask this every wk)

- default = in + out thru the nose, unless I cue a sigh out the mouth
- nose warms + moistens + filters the air (little hairs, mucus) - say it simply
- nose is narrower so breath is naturally slower -> easier to keep it smooth + make exhale longer
- in poses: if breath gets ragged/mouth opens, thats the sign to ease off
- stuffy nose -> mouth is FINE. don't make it a rule that stresses ppl out
- some ppl online say nose breathing = 20% more oxygen?? not sure where that's from, leave out?
- practice: lie down, hand on belly, 5 slow nose breaths
- dizzy = sit, breathe normal
