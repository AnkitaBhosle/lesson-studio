## Source: memo
ok knife safety for saturday, keep it short, they're home cooks not line cooks.

claw grip first. fingertips curled under, knuckles forward, blade rides on the knuckles. show it slow, from the side, and from above. this is the one thing I want them to remember.

sharp knife is safer than dull, dull slips. damp towel under the board so it doesn't skate.

if it falls, step back, don't grab it.

walking, point down by your leg.

wash it right away, not in the sink full of bubbles.

small cut, press a clean towel on it, hold your hand up, tell me.
