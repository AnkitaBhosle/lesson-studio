## Source
pres perfect - Level 3 eve class (Mon/Wed)

FORM: have/has + past participle
 I have worked / she has eaten / they haven't called
 irregulars = the hard part. go-gone, see-seen, eat-eaten, write-written, be-been. give them the list again (the green one)

USES (3, keep it 3!!)
1. life experience, no time said. ever / never
 - Have you ever been to Canada?
 - I've never tried sushi.
 - She has visited Mexico three times.
2. started past + still true now. for / since
 - for = length (for six years, for a long time)
 - since = start point (since 2019, since I was a kid)
 - I've lived here for six years. / since 2019.
3. just happened / result now. just, already, yet
 - I've just finished.
 - We've already eaten.
 - Have you done it yet? / I haven't done it yet. (yet = ? and neg only)

!! finished time word -> SIMPLE PAST
 yesterday, last week, in 2019, two days ago
 X I have seen him yesterday
 ✓ I saw him yesterday
 (Spanish + Portuguese speakers do this a lot, the French speakers too)

been vs gone - students mix up. she has been to Paris / she has gone to Paris. (explain?? next class maybe)

speaking activity: Find someone who... (has ever...) - 10 min
