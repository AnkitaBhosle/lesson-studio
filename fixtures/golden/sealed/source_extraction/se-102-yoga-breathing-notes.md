## Source
breathing basics, wk 1 (beginners, the tues/thurs 6pm group)

- in thru nose, out thru nose unless I say otherwise. nose warms + moistens the air
- start w/ belly breath. one hand belly one hand chest -> belly hand moves more. lying down = easiest, do it on the mat first
- count: in 4 / out 6. longer exhale = calmer (thats what ppl notice most, they always comment)
- ujjayi LATER not wk1!! newbies strain + get red in the face
- NEVER hold breath in a pose. if u can't breathe smooth, back off the pose
- dizzy -> stop, normal breathing, sit down
- stuffy nose / cold = mouth is fine, dont force it
- remind them there's no "right" breath this wk, just notice it
- blanket under knees for the lying down part (bolsters are in the back closet)
