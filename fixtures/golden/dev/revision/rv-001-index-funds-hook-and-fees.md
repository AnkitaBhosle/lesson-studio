## Base plan
Story: Start with the instinct to pick winners, then flip it: you can own the whole haystack instead.

1. Hook — Most people think investing means finding the next big stock. What if you bought the whole haystack instead?
2. What an index fund is — An index is a list of companies; an index fund buys everything on the list.
3. Why low fees matter — Index funds usually cost less to run, and small yearly costs add up.
4. What an expense ratio is — The yearly cost has a name: a percentage of what you've invested.
5. A real example — One purchase made me part-owner of hundreds of companies.
6. The common mistake — Selling when the market drops.
7. Recap and next step — Buy the haystack, keep costs low, stay in. Disclaimer: education, not individual investment advice.
